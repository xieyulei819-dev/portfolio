补充件 ① · 房产账号内容复盘报告（练习件）

Appendix 1 · Property account content review (personal exercise)

  • 房源实拍类停留短，需前 3 秒交代位置与总价区间；房价解析类停留更稳、适合收藏；谈判纪实类最耗时，但信任建立最直接。
  • Walkthroughs had shorter watch time and need location plus price range in the first 3 seconds; price analysis held attention better; negotiation scenes took the most effort but built trust fastest.
  • 问题：发布节奏不稳、开头缺模板、复盘未记录选题来源与拍摄成本。
  • Issues: unstable cadence, no standard opening template, reviews without topic source or filming cost.
  • 改进：30 条选题库（三类各 10 条）；开头模板 A/B 对照（数字开头 vs 冲突开头）；复盘表增加选题来源与拍摄耗时，用「停留时长 ÷ 拍摄耗时」看性价比。
  • Next: a 30-topic bank (10 per format); A/B tests of openings (numbers vs conflict); review sheet extended with topic source and filming time, using watch time ÷ filming time as an efficiency read.

补充件 ② · 内容营销提案（练习提案 · 未实际投放）

Appendix 2 · Content marketing proposal (exercise, never executed)

假设对象：国货护肤品牌「谷雨」。目标：用 30 天内容周期，把内容从「讲成分」转向「讲场景」。人群与洞察：22–30 岁一线/新一线女性，熬夜、换季、日晒导致的泛红暗沉；她们不缺成分知识，缺的是「我的问题该用哪支、怎么用、多久见效」的判断。

Assumed subject: a Chinese skincare brand (Gu Yu). Goal: over a 30-day cycle, shift content from explaining ingredients to solving scenarios. Audience: women 22–30 in tier-1 cities; they do not lack ingredient knowledge — they lack guidance on which product fits their own problem, how to use it and how soon to expect change.

平台分工：小红书＝场景解决方案（问题 + 流程 + 28 天记录）；抖音＝成分与信任（研发视角 + 误区拆解，3 秒给结论）；评论区 FAQ 反哺选题。衡量与合规：停留 / 完播 / 收藏 / 问题密度 + 搜索量、加购、复购；功效表述按备案范围，不承诺疗效，不贬损竞品。

Channels: Xiaohongshu = scenario solutions (problem + routine + 28-day record); Douyin = ingredients and trust (R&D view, myth-busting, conclusion in 3 seconds); comment FAQs feed back into topics. Measurement and compliance: watch time, completion, saves and question density, plus search, add-to-cart and repurchase; efficacy claims kept within regulatory scope, no promised cures, no disparaging competitors.
